MatchMates vs CricHeroes

CricHeroes is an excellent product. It solved cricket scorekeeping for organized leagues and clubs. MatchMates solves a different problem: the complete operational layer for a community that plays multiple sports informally, all year round.

The Core Difference

CricHeroes is a cricket scorekeeping platform. It is built to record cricket matches played in structured leagues and tournaments, generate detailed cricket statistics, and give individual players a cricket-specific profile.

MatchMates is a community sports operating system. It is built to manage the complete sporting life of a residential community — across cricket, badminton, pickleball, football, table tennis, and other sports — including scheduling, facilities, player discovery, rankings, and the full match lifecycle.

These are different products solving different problems. The right choice depends on what your community actually needs.

The Two Friction Points CricHeroes Doesn't Solve for Local Cricket

Adding players is instant on MatchMates

In CricHeroes, getting 22 players into a match requires the organiser to search for each player individually by phone number — and every player must already have the CricHeroes app installed. For a casual apartment cricket game with a mix of regular and occasional players, this is a significant barrier. Players who don't have the app simply don't get added.

In MatchMates, the organiser shares a single WhatsApp link. Players tap it to join. No searching, no manual entry, no prerequisite installations. The entire team is assembled in the time it takes to forward one message.

Live scoring is viewable without any download

Following a live CricHeroes scorecard requires the CricHeroes app. For spectators, family members, or players waiting to bat — anyone who wants to follow the match in real time needs to install and set up the app first.

MatchMates live scorecards open in any browser. Share the scorecard link on WhatsApp and anyone can follow ball by ball on their phone — no download, no account, no friction. The match comes to the audience, not the other way around.

When CricHeroes Is the Right Choice

CricHeroes excels when cricket is the only sport your group plays, the primary need is detailed ball-by-ball cricket statistics, you're running structured club-level or academy-level leagues, and you want integration with the broader CricHeroes community and discovery features for cricket players across cities.

If your apartment complex has a dedicated cricket club that participates in inter-society tournaments, CricHeroes handles the scoring and tournament statistics extremely well.

When MatchMates Is the Right Choice

MatchMates is designed for communities where the sports coordinator needs to manage more than one sport, match scheduling and player availability are as important as scorekeeping, the community plays informally and year-round (not just in a formal tournament season), and the sports history needs to survive admin changes and player relocations.

If your residential community has 200 residents playing badminton on weekends, cricket in the evenings, and pickleball when the court is free — MatchMates is built for this reality. CricHeroes is not.

Can You Use Both?

Yes. Some communities use CricHeroes for inter-society cricket tournaments (where cricket statistics and external visibility matter) and MatchMates for their day-to-day internal sports operations across multiple sports. The two platforms serve different audiences and different timelines within the same community's sports life.

CricHeroes answers: "What happened in our cricket league?"
MatchMates answers: "What is our entire community's sporting life — across all sports, all year, for every resident?"

The Category Difference

CricHeroes built a vertical for cricket. It owns cricket scorekeeping in India for amateur and club-level games. That is a strong and defensible position.

MatchMates is building a horizontal — a platform layer for the full community sports experience. The goal is not to beat CricHeroes at cricket. It is to make MatchMates the default infrastructure for a community's sporting identity, across all sports, regardless of which sport is most popular in that community today.

Think of the difference between a restaurant booking app (vertical: restaurants) and a calendar app (horizontal: all appointments). CricHeroes is to cricket what OpenTable is to restaurants. MatchMates is to community sports what Google Calendar is to scheduling — a layer under everything.
