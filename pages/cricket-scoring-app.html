What Makes a Good Cricket Scoring App for Local Cricket?

Most cricket scoring apps are built for formal club cricket — structured leagues, registered players, dedicated scorers with time to set up every match from scratch. Local cricket is different. You have 22 people, a ground, an hour to play, and nobody wants to spend 20 minutes adding players before the toss.

A cricket scoring app for local cricket needs to do three things well:

  • Start fast — players joined, teams formed, toss done in under 5 minutes
  • Score without friction — one tap per ball, automatic totals, no mental arithmetic mid-over
  • Mean something after — every match adds to a player's permanent record, not a scorecard that disappears

MatchMates is the cricket scoring app for apartment tournaments, weekend cricket, Maidan cricket, and corporate cricket — anywhere informal cricket happens regularly and players deserve more than a forgotten WhatsApp message as their match record.

Features Built for the Cricket Scorer

Live ball-by-ball scoring

Tap the outcome for each delivery — runs, wickets, wides, no-balls, byes. MatchMates calculates totals, run rates, required rates, and partnerships automatically. The scorer focuses on the game; the app handles the arithmetic.

Live scorecard viewable without download

Share the scorecard link on WhatsApp before the match starts. Every player, spectator, and family member can follow ball by ball in any browser — no app download required to watch.

Players join via WhatsApp link

No manual player entry. No searching by phone number. The organiser shares one WhatsApp link — players tap to join. Teams of 22 assembled in seconds, not 20 minutes.

Features Built for the Player

Personal milestones

First half-century. First five-wicket haul. 50 matches played. MatchMates tracks personal milestones automatically and marks them permanently on each player's profile. The milestone you hit in a Sunday apartment match counts — because now it's recorded forever.

Unlimited personal history

Every match adds to your career record — runs, wickets, averages, strike rates, economy rates — building across months and years. No other cricket scoring app for apartment cricket builds this kind of permanent personal record for informal games.

ELO player ratings and rankings

Your ELO rating updates after every match. The community leaderboard updates live. Players have a reason to perform every time they play.

Portable career record

Move to a new apartment or city? Your cricket history travels with you. Join a new community on MatchMates and your full career record is immediately visible. No starting from zero.

Features Built for the Organiser

AI-balanced team formation

MatchMates reads each player's ELO rating and suggests balanced teams automatically. No more captain's bias, no more stacked sides. Fair teams in seconds — and closer matches as a result.

Game scheduling

Create a match, set the date, time, and ground. The organiser sees confirmed, pending, and declined players in one view — no chasing WhatsApp confirmations.

Tournament management

Running a society cricket tournament? MatchMates generates fixtures automatically for round robin, knockout, or league formats. Standings update live. No spreadsheet needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is MatchMates free?

Yes. Free for live scoring, personal history, rankings, and tournament management.

Do all players need to download the app?

Live scorecards are viewable in any browser without downloading. Players tracking personal history and rankings need to download the app.

Does it work for short formats like 6-over or 8-over cricket?

Yes. MatchMates works for any over format — 6, 8, 10, 20, or unlimited overs.

How is it different from CricHeroes?

In CricHeroes, every player must be manually added by phone number and must have the app installed. In MatchMates, players join via a WhatsApp link instantly. Live scorecards are viewable without any download. See the full MatchMates vs CricHeroes comparison.
