What Makes a Good Cricket Scoring App for Local Cricket?

Most cricket scoring apps are built for formal club cricket — structured leagues, registered players, dedicated scorers with time to set up every match from scratch. Local cricket is different. You have 22 people, a ground, an hour to play, and nobody wants to spend 20 minutes adding players before the toss.

A cricket scoring app for local cricket needs to do three things well:

  • Start fast — players joined, teams formed, toss done in under 5 minutes
  • Score without friction — one tap per ball, automatic totals, no mental arithmetic mid-over
  • Mean something after — every match adds to a player's permanent record, not a scorecard that disappears

MatchMates is built around all three.

MatchMates is the cricket scoring app that works for apartment tournaments, weekend cricket, Maidan cricket, and corporate cricket — anywhere informal cricket happens regularly and players deserve more than a forgotten WhatsApp message as their match record.

Features Built for the Cricket Scorer

Live ball-by-ball scoring

Tap the outcome for each delivery — runs, wickets, wides, no-balls, byes. MatchMates calculates totals, run rates, required rates, and partnerships automatically. The scorer focuses on the game; the app handles the arithmetic.

Live scorecard viewable without download

Share the scorecard link on WhatsApp before the match starts. Every player, spectator, and family member can follow ball by ball in any browser — no app download required to watch. The crowd stays connected to the game even off the ground.

Players join via WhatsApp link

No manual player entry. No searching by phone number. The organiser shares one WhatsApp link — players tap to join the match. Teams of 22 assembled in seconds, not 20 minutes.

Features Built for the Player

Personal milestones

First half-century. First five-wicket haul. 50 matches played. 100 runs in a season. MatchMates tracks personal milestones automatically and marks them permanently on each player's profile. The milestone you hit in a Sunday apartment match counts — because now it's recorded forever.

Unlimited personal history

Every match you play on MatchMates adds to your career record. Runs, wickets, averages, strike rates, economy rates — building across months and years. When you look back at your profile in 3 years, you'll see every match, every performance, every season. No other cricket scoring app for apartment cricket builds this kind of permanent personal record for informal games.

ELO player ratings and rankings

Your skill rating updates after every match based on who you played against and what the result was. The community leaderboard updates live. Players know exactly where they stand — and have a reason to perform every time they play.

Portable career record

Move to a new apartment or city? Your cricket history travels with you. Join a new community on MatchMates and your full career record is immediately visible to the new organiser. No starting from zero. No having to prove yourself again.

Features Built for the Organiser

AI-balanced team formation

MatchMates reads each player's ELO rating and suggests balanced teams automatically. No more captain's bias, no more stacked sides, no more 20-minute arguments about who goes where. Fair teams in seconds — and closer matches as a result.

Game scheduling

Create a match, set the date, time, and ground. Players confirm availability directly. The organiser sees confirmed, pending, and declined in one view — no chasing individual WhatsApp confirmations through a 200-message thread.

Tournament management

Running a society cricket tournament? MatchMates generates fixtures automatically for round robin, knockout, or league formats. Standings update live after every match result. No spreadsheet needed from setup to trophy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is MatchMates free?

Yes. MatchMates is free to use for live scoring, personal history, rankings, and tournament management.

Do all players need to download the app?

Live scorecards are viewable in any browser without downloading anything. Players who want to track their personal history, milestones, and rankings need to download the app.

Does it work for short formats like 6-over or 8-over cricket?

Yes. MatchMates works for any over format — 6, 8, 10, 20, or unlimited overs. Set the format when you create the match.

Can I use it without an internet connection at the ground?

A basic data connection is needed for live scoring. Most urban grounds in India have sufficient 4G coverage for the app to work without issues.

How is it different from CricHeroes?

In CricHeroes, every player must be manually added by phone number and must have the app installed. In MatchMates, players join via a WhatsApp link — no admin entry needed. Live scorecards are also viewable without any download on MatchMates. See the full MatchMates vs CricHeroes comparison.
