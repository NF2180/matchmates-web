Dimension 1

In-Game Scoring &
Rule Flexibility

The core engine — whether the app handles the real realities of informal and formal cricket, from 6-over box cricket to 20-over weekend games.

Custom Match Formats Live

Configure any over count — 6, 8, 10, 15, 20, or unlimited. Set custom player counts (8v8, 11v11, or any combination). Works for box cricket, apartment matches, and full-length games without any format restrictions.

Wide & No Ball — 1 Run + Reball Live

Standard extras rule enforced automatically. Wide or No Ball adds 1 run to the team total and the delivery does not count as a valid ball — it is automatically added to the over. No manual over-length tracking needed.

Bye & Leg Bye Live

Tap Bye or Leg Bye, enter the run count. Credited to the team total, not the batsman's score or the bowler's figures. Treated as a valid delivery — over count advances normally.

Wicket Recording Live

One-tap Wicket button records any dismissal type. Updates bowler figures, advances the batting lineup, and prompts next batsman selection. Wicket is recorded against the bowler's economy and strike rate.

Multi-Step Undo Live

Undo any number of previous deliveries — not just the last ball. Reverses runs, extras, and wickets without corrupting match state. Essential for correcting scorer errors on a live boundary line.

Edit Any Previous Ball Live

Open any past delivery and correct the recorded outcome — runs, extras, or dismissal type. Match state recalculates automatically from the edited point forward. No need to restart the innings for a single entry error.

Compulsory Retirement Rules Coming Soon

Auto-trigger retirement at a configurable run threshold (e.g., retire at 30 runs in box cricket). App will alert the scorer when the threshold is reached and prompt the next batsman. Common in apartment and corporate cricket.

Mid-Match Player Substitution Coming Soon

Replace a player mid-innings without corrupting batting or bowling figures. Substitutes will be tracked separately from original squad members in the scorecard and career stats.

Advanced Dismissal Types Coming Soon

Specific dismissal logging — Mankad, Retired Out, Retired Hurt, Timed Out, Obstructing the Field. Currently all dismissals are recorded as Wicket. Dismissal type detail and fielder attribution coming in a future build.

Dimension 2

Data Infrastructure &
Connectivity

How the app handles network drops on outdoor grounds, delivers live scores to spectators, and builds long-term player analytics.

Live Scorecard — No Download to View Live

Every match generates a live scorecard URL. Share it on WhatsApp before the first ball. Family, friends, and players waiting to bat can follow ball-by-ball in any browser — no app install required. Zero friction for spectators.

Career Statistics — Auto-Aggregated Live

Every match automatically adds to each player's permanent career record: total matches, runs, wickets, batting average, strike rate, bowling economy. Aggregated across all communities — not reset between seasons or groups.

ELO Skill Ratings — Live Update Live

Each player's skill rating updates the moment a match result is entered. Ratings account for opposition strength — beating a higher-rated team earns more points. Community leaderboard refreshes automatically after every match.

Offline Scoring — Sync Later Coming Soon

Score a complete match 100% offline on a Maidan or ground with no signal. Ball-by-ball data cached locally. Auto-syncs to cloud — career stats, leaderboards, match history — the moment a connection is restored. No data lost.

Advanced Analytics — Wagon Wheel, Manhattan Coming Soon

Visual match analytics including Wagon Wheel (shot direction), Manhattan chart (runs per over), partnership graphs, run-rate progression, and bowler economy splits by over. Currently basic scorecard only.

Scorecard Export — PDF & WhatsApp Image Coming Soon

Export completed scorecards as a clean PDF or shareable WhatsApp graphic — team scores, individual batting and bowling figures, match result, player of the match. One tap to share the full match summary to any group.

Dimension 3

League & Community
Management

The macro layer — from organising a single match to running a full tournament with brackets, standings, and player administration.

Player Join via WhatsApp Link Live

Share one link — players tap to join the match. No manual entry by phone number. No prerequisite app install to be added to a squad. 22 players added in the time it takes to forward one WhatsApp message.

AI-Balanced Team Formation Live

MatchMates reads each player's current ELO rating and automatically suggests balanced Team A and Team B. No captain bias, no stacked sides. Captains can swap players manually if needed. Fair teams in seconds.

Player Availability & RSVP Live

Create a match, set the date and ground. Players confirm or decline from the WhatsApp invite. Organiser sees confirmed, pending, and declined in one view — no chasing individual messages through a 200-message thread.

Tournament Fixture Generation Live

Create a round robin, knockout, or league tournament. MatchMates generates the complete fixture list automatically. No manual bracket drawing. Results entered after each match update standings in real time.

Live Tournament Standings Live

Points table updates automatically after every match result is entered. Standings visible to all tournament participants without organiser intervention. No spreadsheet, no manual update required.

Persistent Player Identity Across Communities Live

A player's career record, ELO rating, and match history follow them when they move to a new apartment or join a new cricket group. No starting from zero. The organiser of any new community can see exactly who they're getting.

Net Run Rate Auto-Calculation Coming Soon

NRR calculated and displayed automatically in tournament standings. Currently points only. NRR tiebreaker will be added as part of the tournament standings upgrade — essential for multi-team round robin tournaments.

Match Media — Photos & Highlights Coming Soon

Upload match photos to the scorecard. AI-generated match highlight text and auto-overlay scorecard graphics for social sharing. Coming as part of the post-match experience upgrade.

Dimension 4

Field Usability &
Performance

How the app performs in real outdoor conditions — sunlight, heat, low battery, and the speed required to score ball by ball without delaying the game.

1–2 Taps Per Ball Live

Standard deliveries scored in one tap — select the run count (0–6) on a single screen. Extras require one additional tap (Wide, No Ball, Bye, Leg Bye) then run count. Wicket is one tap. The scorer never leaves the main scoring screen mid-over.

Dark Mode — High Contrast UI Live

MatchMates is built on a dark background with high-contrast green and white elements. The scoring interface — large run buttons, bold score display — is designed for readability in direct sunlight. No separate light/dark toggle needed.

Lightweight — Battery & Thermal Efficient Live

No background video, no heavy animations, no constant polling. MatchMates is a lean web app that does not tax the CPU or drain the battery faster than a typical messaging app. Suitable for a full 3–4 hour match on a single charge.

5 Clicks to First Ball Live

From opening the app: Create Match → Add Players → Teams & Toss → Innings Setup → Start Scoring. Five screens. Under 5 minutes from app open to live ball entry — the fastest setup of any cricket scoring app for informal cricket.

Works on Any Smartphone Browser Live

The live scorecard requires no app download for spectators — it opens in Chrome, Safari, or any mobile browser. Scorer and organisers use the app. Everyone else follows on a browser link. No barriers to entry for the crowd.

Offline Mode — Score Without Signal Coming Soon

Full offline scoring capability — cache all ball data locally when signal drops. Auto-sync to cloud once connection is restored. No match data lost on a Maidan with patchy 4G or an indoor ground with no coverage.
