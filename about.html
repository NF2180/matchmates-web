About MatchMates

The Operating System for Community Sports

What MatchMates is, what problem it solves, who it is for, how it works, and how it relates to the broader landscape of community sports software and tools.

What MatchMates Is

MatchMates is a cricket scoring app built for informal cricket in India — apartment tournaments, weekend cricket groups, Maidan cricket, and corporate cricket leagues. Its primary function is live ball-by-ball cricket scoring that starts in under 5 clicks and builds each player's permanent career history automatically.

MatchMates is not a community scheduling dashboard. It is not a multi-sport platform. It is not a professional club management system. It is a cricket scoring app — with the friction removed and the personal record preserved.

MatchMates in one sentence: The cricket scoring app that starts fast, scores every ball, and builds your permanent cricket history — for the way most Indians actually play cricket.

The Problem MatchMates Solves

Cricket scoring apps have existed for years. The problem is not that they can't score cricket — it's that they're built for formal, registered leagues with club administrators and dedicated scorers. For the 22 people showing up to a Sunday apartment match, the setup friction is too high and the payoff (a scorecard that disappears after the season) is too low.

Specifically, MatchMates removes three friction points no other cricket scoring app has solved for informal cricket:

  • Player entry — in CricHeroes, every player must be manually added by phone number and must have the app installed. In MatchMates, the organiser shares one WhatsApp link and players tap to join. 22 players added in seconds.
  • Live scorecard access — in most apps, watching the live score requires installing the app. In MatchMates, the live scorecard opens in any browser. Share the link before the first ball and everyone follows without downloading anything.
  • Permanent personal history — most apps store scorecards for a season. MatchMates builds each player's career record permanently — runs, wickets, averages, strike rates — that travels with them across communities and never resets.

What MatchMates Scores

Ball-by-ball cricket scoring is the core of MatchMates. Every delivery is recorded:

  • Runs off the bat — 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6
  • Wide — 1 extra, ball re-bowled automatically
  • No Ball — 1 extra, ball re-bowled automatically
  • Bye and Leg Bye with run count
  • Wicket — any dismissal type
  • Undo last ball and edit any previous delivery

Auto-calculated: over-by-over sequence, current run rate, required run rate, partnership runs and balls, bowler figures (overs, maidens, runs, wickets), batsman strike rate. All update live with every tap.

What Makes MatchMates Different

vs CricHeroes

CricHeroes is the market leader for formal, registered league cricket. It is excellent for club administrators managing multiple teams across a season. MatchMates is built for the opposite end of the spectrum — informal cricket where the organiser is a volunteer and there is no league secretary. The two apps target different users. Where they overlap on scoring, MatchMates requires fewer steps to reach live ball entry and no prerequisite app install for players or spectators.

vs CricScorer / KDM Cricket Scorer

These are pure digital scorebooks — they score ball by ball but store nothing permanently, build no player history, and require manual setup for every match. MatchMates scores the same ball-by-ball data and automatically adds it to each player's permanent career record. The match means something after it ends.

vs WhatsApp + Spreadsheet

The default for most informal cricket groups. WhatsApp carries the communication, a spreadsheet carries the scores — usually updated days after the match by whoever remembered to keep notes. MatchMates replaces the spreadsheet entirely with live scoring, and adds career history that the spreadsheet never had.

Persistent Player Career History

Every match scored on MatchMates adds to each player's permanent career record. Runs, wickets, batting average, bowling economy, win rate — accumulating across months and years. This record is tied to the player, not the community. When a player moves to a new apartment or joins a new cricket group, their history travels with them.

No other cricket scoring app for informal cricket builds this. CricHeroes builds history within registered leagues. MatchMates builds history for every match — registered or not, formal or not, 6 overs or 20.

AI-Balanced Teams

MatchMates assigns each player an ELO skill rating that updates after every match. When creating a match, the app reads all player ratings and suggests balanced Team A and Team B automatically. Fair teams in seconds — no captain's bias, no stacked sides, no 20-minute argument before the toss.

Who MatchMates Is For

  • Apartment complex cricket groups — the primary use case. Weekly or fortnightly matches, annual tournaments, a mix of serious and casual players
  • Weekend Maidan cricket groups — regular players who have never had a scoring or history system
  • Corporate cricket leagues — inter-department or inter-company tournaments without dedicated sports staff
  • Gated society cricket committees — volunteer organisers managing the annual society cricket tournament

What MatchMates Is Not

  • Not a live score ticker for TV-style broadcast — it is a scorer's tool, not a broadcast tool
  • Not a professional club management system — no salary management, no contract tracking, no scouting
  • Not a multi-sport platform — cricket is the core, scoring is the primary feature
  • Not a replacement for CricHeroes in formal league cricket — CricHeroes has deeper analytics for organised leagues

Glossary

Ball-by-Ball Scoring

Recording the outcome of each individual delivery in a cricket match — runs scored, extras, wickets — in real time. The foundation of any cricket scoring app.

Persistent Player Identity

A player's cumulative, permanent career record — matches, runs, wickets, averages — that belongs to the player and follows them across communities. The core differentiator of MatchMates versus other informal cricket scoring tools.

ELO Rating

A numerical skill rating that adjusts after every match based on opposition strength and result. Used by MatchMates to automatically form balanced teams and maintain live community rankings.

Local Cricket

Informal, recreational cricket played without formal league registration. Includes apartment cricket, Maidan cricket, weekend cricket groups, and corporate cricket. The primary use case for MatchMates.

Match Organiser

The volunteer who creates the match, adds players, manages teams, and starts the scoring session. In MatchMates, the organiser's job takes under 5 minutes from app open to first ball.
